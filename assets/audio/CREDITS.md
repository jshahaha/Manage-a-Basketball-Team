# Audio files & credits

These are the first-pass sounds for the game, as `.ogg` files ready to upload to Roblox. Upload them in Studio with **Asset Manager → Bulk Import**, then paste each asset ID into `src/shared/Sounds.lua` (files named `Name_1`, `Name_2` and so on are variants of `Name`).

## Sources and licenses
- **BigSoundBank** recordings are CC0 (public domain): free to use commercially, with no credit needed. Credit is given here anyway. https://bigsoundbank.com
- **Commentator** lines were generated with **Kokoro** (open-source text-to-speech, Apache 2.0 license, voice `am_michael`), which allows commercial use.
- **Synth** sounds were made from scratch for this game.

## Still missing
